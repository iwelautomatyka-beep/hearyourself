Simple Setup

Ready in a few seconds.

  1. Connect wired headphones (3.5 mm jack or USB-C).
  2. Open the Monitor screen.
  3. Start live monitor — hear yourself instantly.
  4. Pick a Vocal Style or stack effects manually.
  5. Tap REC to capture an M4A take on-device.

Why wired only?

Live monitoring is all about the round-trip time from your mouth to your ears. Wired headphones keep that path under ~50 ms; Bluetooth typically adds 150–300 ms, which is too late for real-time vocal feedback. If you only want to record (no live monitor), the app has a Record-only mode that works without headphones.
