2. App purpose

HearYourself is intended as a low-latency live vocal monitor tool with built-in studio-style voice effects (compressor, de-esser, shimmer reverb, doubler), 14 vocal style presets, a three-band Vocal EQ, and on-device M4A voice recording. It is intended for creative, practice, and audio-monitoring use.

3. Intended setup

For the live monitor, the app is intended for use with wired headphones (3.5 mm jack or USB-C) and the phone microphone. Bluetooth headphones are not supported for live monitoring because their typical 150–300 ms latency is too high for real-time vocal feedback. The Record-only mode can be used without headphones. Actual latency, audio quality, and routing behavior can vary depending on the Android device, operating system, audio path, and connected accessories.

4. User responsibility

  • Use safe listening levels. Live monitoring can become loud quickly; start at a low monitor level and raise gradually.
  • Use the app responsibly and in a way that does not risk hearing discomfort.
  • You are responsible for what you record. Do not record people without their knowledge or consent where local law requires it.
  • Check compatibility on your own device before relying on the app in any important situation.

5. Recordings storage

Recordings are stored locally on the device in the app's private storage. Each recording is capped at 60 minutes. Total recordings storage is capped at roughly 100 MB; the oldest files are automatically deleted when the cap is reached. Recordings are removed when the app is uninstalled. The app does not provide a built-in cloud backup; if you want to keep a recording long-term, export it before uninstalling.
